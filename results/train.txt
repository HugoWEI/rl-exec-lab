sigma = rolling std of step log-returns; V = rolling mean step volume (both use past bars only).

  [ON ] spread       : fixed half-spread = 0.5 bps
  [ON ] temp impact  : eta * sigma * (q/V)^delta, eta=1, delta=0.5  (eta is an assumption, not calibrated)
  [OFF] perm impact  : later prices shift by gamma * (qty already bought / prev-day volume), gamma=0.05  (counterfactual: history never saw these trades)
